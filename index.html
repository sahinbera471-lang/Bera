What we do

Most instruments come to us with a dead voice, a tired power supply or keys that stopped feeling right. This is what we can do about it.

  • Repair and restoration

    Fault finding, repairs, calibration and complete refurbishing for instruments that have a few decades behind them.

    Full service, in-house

  • Power supplies

    Our own replacement PSUs, engineered and built in Germany. Adjustable voltages, extra fuses and safety diodes, and much less heat inside the case.

    Universal AC, 88–264 V

  • Keybeds

    New Fatar TP/9 keybeds as plug-and-play kits. Aftertouch on request.

    Memorymoog · Prophet VS · Matrix 12

  • MIDI and memory

    MIDI boards for synths that never had MIDI, and memory expansions with up to 1,600 presets for the Prophet series.

    Minimoog · 4 Voice · Prophets

  • Mods

    Filter and audio mods, poly mod for the Matrix 12, pot upgrades, new displays and an SD emulator for the DPX-1.

    OB-8 · Matrix 12 · DPX-1
